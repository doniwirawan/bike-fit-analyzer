# Surface brief: bikefit.doniwirawan.xyz (landing, analyzer, guides, legal)

Scope and visitor mode: landing `web/index.html` is Persuade; analyzer `web/app.html` is Operate; `web/blog/*` and `privacy.html` / `terms.html` are Read. One shared world across all of them via `web/base.css`.

Audience and job: home cyclists (EN/ID) checking saddle height and fit from a phone clip before changing parts or paying a fitter. Action: open the analyzer and run a clip. Proof on hand: the analyzer itself, the sample report, one real rider post on Threads, cited targets. Constraints: privacy promise (no third-party loads), EN/ID parity, app.html DOM hooks, soft reach advice.

Chosen direction: the category standard (user chose canon), craft bar Strava, Whoop/Oura, Linear/Vercel, Apple Fitness/Health.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a consumer sports-health product, played straight at Strava/Whoop/Apple Health craft: the product report leads, numbers are big, colour only ever means a grade. It refuses the SaaS template kit: eyebrow labels, fake browser chrome, icon-tile card grids, chip rows, glow gradients, a four-column link footer.

OWN-WORLD: Cool near-white ground, near-black ink, hairline borders, 14px cards, no drop shadows except the one elevated report. The logo's blue is the single brand accent (primary action, links, focus). Green/amber/red are reserved for grades. Mona Sans variable: expanded, heavy for display and tabular numerals; normal width for text. Dark theme mirrors it on a graphite ground.

STORY: The rider sees a real graded report first, understands it is measured from their own clip on their own device, trusts it because the page states its limits, and taps Analyse a clip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: the H1 at display scale in expanded heavy, one-sentence sub, primary button "Analyse a clip" plus a secondary Android link, one privacy line. Right column, wider: the sample report as a product card, the figure on top, the knee number as the largest numeral on the page, three metric rows each with a range bar and a worded grade.

FORM: canon (standing exit, chosen over the roll); seed:125f95eb2fcb7e33. Signature interaction: focusing or hovering a metric row highlights its limb on the figure; range-bar markers settle once on first view (static under reduced motion).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Unresolved: none.
